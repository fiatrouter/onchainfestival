Questions

Answered

Who attends Onchain Festival?+

Founders, investors, government officials, regulators, enterprise leaders, builders, media, and the entire ecosystem of Africa's digital economy. In 2025, attendees came from 24 countries and included representatives from SEC Nigeria, LSETF, Coinbase, Lisk, Aptos, Zenith Bank, and Wema Bank.

When and where is Onchain Festival 2026?+

October 30, 2026 in Lagos, Nigeria. The exact venue will be confirmed and announced this year. Sign up to our mailing list to be notified first.

How do I become a speaker?+

Send your speaker application to femiadegolu@gmail.com with the subject line "Speaker Application." We prioritise speakers who bring real data, real decisions, and a perspective the audience will not hear anywhere else. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

What sponsorship packages are available?+

We offer six tiers from Title Partner down to Startup Partner. Book a call with Femi at the link on this page and we will walk through the right option for your brand and objectives.

Is this only about blockchain?+

No. Onchain Festival covers the full breadth of Africa's digital economy: AI, blockchain, digital assets, stablecoins, payments, fintech, compliance, government policy, enterprise adoption, and startup innovation. We deliberately built a cross-sector platform because the future of Africa's digital economy does not sit inside one vertical.

Where do I buy a ticket?+

Tickets are available at https://www.gruve.events/onchain-festival-2-0. Early registration is open now and we recommend securing your spot early as the 2025 edition sold out.
